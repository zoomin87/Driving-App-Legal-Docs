2. What Biting Point is

Biting Point is a scheduling tool. It helps driving instructors and learner drivers find each other, share availability and arrange lessons.

Biting Point is not a driving school. We do not provide driving lessons, and we do not employ, recommend or endorse any instructor. We are not a party to any agreement between an instructor and a learner. Every lesson is arranged directly between the instructor and the learner, who are responsible for it.

3. Your account

  • You must be 18 or over to use Biting Point.
  • You sign up as either an instructor or a learner. You must choose the role that is true for you.
  • The information in your profile must be accurate and kept up to date.
  • Each person may have only one account. Do not create an account for someone else or let anyone else use yours.
  • Keep your password confidential. You are responsible for what happens under your account. Tell us straight away if you think someone else has used it.
  • You must verify your email address with the code we send you before you can use your account.

4. If you are an instructor

  • You must be legally allowed to give paid driving instruction: either an Approved Driving Instructor (ADI), or a Potential Driving Instructor (PDI) with a valid trainee licence.
  • Your instructor type, badge number and badge expiry date must be true and kept up to date. If your registration or trainee licence is suspended, revoked or expires, update your profile and tell us.
  • You alone are responsible for your lessons, including making sure your vehicle is roadworthy, taxed, has a valid MOT and is suitably equipped; holding insurance that covers driving tuition; the prices you charge; your own tax and legal obligations; and your learners' safety during lessons.
  • You may use a learner's details, including their mobile number, only to arrange and give lessons, and for nothing else.

5. Badge checks

An instructor can email us a photo of their badge. We may check it by hand and then show "Badge checked by Biting Point" on their profile.

This only means that, when we checked, the badge photo appeared to match the details on the profile. It is not an approval or endorsement by the DVSA or by us. It does not guarantee the instructor's identity, qualifications, conduct or ability. A check stops being shown as valid once the badge expiry date has passed.

Learners should always check the instructor's badge in the car windscreen before a lesson starts.

6. If you are a learner

  • Before driving, you must hold a valid provisional or full driving licence and meet the legal eyesight standard.
  • Keep your availability, test date and lesson details accurate.
  • Before your first lesson, check your instructor's badge and agree the price, payment and cancellation arrangements with them.

7. Connections and bookings

  • Instructors can invite learners by email. Learners cannot invite instructors. Learners can only accept or reject instructor's invite, and either of you can disconnect later.
  • Once connected, the instructor can see the learner's availability and book lessons, including recurring lessons.
  • After a disconnect, the instructor can still see their past lessons with that learner but cannot book new ones.
  • A booking in Biting Point is a record in a calendar. Any agreement about a lesson, including cancellations, no-shows and refunds, is between the instructor and the learner. We do not enforce those agreements.

8. Payments

No payments are made through Biting Point. The price on an instructor's profile is set by that instructor and is for information only. Payment is arranged directly between the instructor and the learner, and we are not responsible for any payment or payment dispute.

9. Instructor subscription

Instructor accounts are free for 12 months from the date the account is created. If we plan to introduce a paid plan, we will give you at least 2 months' notice before the change affects you.

10. Offline use and availability of the app

Biting Point works without an internet connection and syncs your changes the next time you are online. This means:

  • information on your screen, such as bookings and availability, may be out of date until your device syncs; and
  • changes you make while offline do not reach the other person until your device syncs.

Confirm important changes, such as a cancelled lesson, directly with the other person.

We try to keep Biting Point running, but we cannot promise it will always be available, uninterrupted or free of errors. We may change, suspend or remove features.

  • send invites or connection requests in bulk, or to people who have not asked for them;
  • try to access accounts or information that are not yours, interfere with the app's security, or get around its limits; or

To report a user, email Support at itsmexcel@gmail.com.

12. Suspending or closing accounts

We may suspend or delete an account that breaks these terms, for example one with false badge details, one impersonating someone, or one used to abuse others. Where it is reasonable to do so, we will tell you why.

You can delete your account at any time in Settings > Delete account. Your personal information is deleted straight away, except booking records, which are kept for 3 years without your name or contact details or any other personal information (see Part B, section 8). Deleting your account cannot be undone.

2. Information we collect

Account (everyone): your email address and password. Your password is handled by our sign-in provider and is never visible to us.

Connections and bookings: who you are connected with and the status of each connection (requested, accepted, or disconnected and when); and each lesson booked, including its times, whether it repeats, its colour, and any title or notes added to it.

Technical information: our server receives your IP address with each request. We use it to protect the service, for example by limiting repeated sign-in attempts. Our hosting providers may keep it in short-term logs.

What we do not collect: we do not collect your location, your contacts, files or photos from your device, your date of birth or your gender. Biting Point contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools. The only device permission the app asks for is internet access. When you tap to call or email someone, your phone's own phone or email app opens. We do not see those calls or emails.

3. Why we use your information, and our lawful basis

  • To provide Biting Point (your account and profile, connections, availability, bookings, syncing and verification emails). Lawful basis: performing our contract with you.
  • To check instructor badges and show learners the result. Lawful basis: legitimate interests, so learners can choose a properly qualified instructor.
  • To keep Biting Point secure (preventing misuse, limiting sign-in attempts, asking for your password before sensitive changes). Lawful basis: legitimate interests.
  • To keep booking records after an account is deleted. Lawful basis: legitimate interests, so the other person keeps an accurate lesson history and so disputes about lessons can be dealt with.
  • To answer your questions and support requests. Lawful basis: legitimate interests.
  • To record which version of this document you accepted. Lawful basis: legitimate interests and legal obligation, to show what you agreed to.
  • To send marketing emails. Lawful basis: consent. We send them only if you ticked the box, and you can withdraw consent at any time in Settings.

4. Who can see your information

Other Biting Point users can see some of your information, depending on your role and your connections.

If you are an instructor:

  • Any signed-in Biting Point learner, can see your listing in the instructor directory: your name, driving school, price per hour, car make and model, transmission, areas covered, instructor type (ADI or PDI), whether your badge has been checked by Biting Point, and your badge expiry date.
  • Any signed-in Biting Point learner can also access your full instructor profile, mobile number, badge number, car details, working days and minimum lesson hours. The app does not show every one of these on screen, but you should not treat any of them as hidden from other users.

If you are a learner:

  • An instructor who has a connection with you, whether the connection is requested, accepted or disconnected, can access your profile, including your name, email address, post code (to pick up for your lessons), mobile number, practical test date if booked, theory test status, previous lessons and preferred transmission, as well as your availability.
  • The app shows mobile numbers for calling only between people who are currently connected.

Bookings: a booking can be seen by the instructor and the learner it involves.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for advertising.

7. Information stored on your device

So that Biting Point works offline, a copy of your information and your sign-in session is stored in the app's private storage on your device. It is deleted when you log out or delete your account, and when you uninstall the app.

8. How long we keep your information

  • While your account is open: we keep your information for as long as you have an account.
  • When you delete your account: your sign-in account, profile, contact details, availability, connections, settings, consent records and directory listing are deleted straight away.
  • Booking records: kept for 3 years after your account is deleted, then permanently deleted. During that time they are linked only to an account ID, not to your name or contact details. The other person in a booking can still see it in their lesson history without your name.
  • Backups: deleted information may remain in our database backups for up to 30 days before it is overwritten.
  • Badge photos: deleted within 30 days of the badge check.
  • Support emails: kept for up to 2 years.
  • Server logs: kept by our hosting providers for a short time only.

9. Keeping your information secure

Biting Point uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). Every request is checked against your signed-in session. Our database has access rules so each user can reach only the information described in section 4. We limit repeated sign-in attempts, and we ask for your password before you change your password or delete your account. No system is completely secure, so please tell us straight away if you think something is wrong.

10. Your rights

  • get a copy of your personal information;
  • have inaccurate information corrected (you can edit your profile in the app yourself);
  • have your information deleted (delete your account in Settings > Delete account, or email us);
  • restrict or object to how we use your information;
  • receive your information in a portable format; and
  • withdraw your consent at any time, for example to marketing emails.

To use any of these rights, email itsmexcel@gmail.com from the email address on your account. It is free, and we will reply within one month. We may need to confirm your identity first. If you ask us to delete booking records earlier than section 8 describes, we will weigh your request against the other person's interest in keeping their lesson history.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please contact us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk, telephone 0303 123 1113.
