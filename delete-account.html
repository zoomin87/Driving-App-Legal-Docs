Delete your Biting Point account

You can delete your Biting Point account and your personal information at any time. Deleting an account cannot be undone.

From inside the app

Open Biting Point and go to Settings > Delete account. You will be asked for your password, and then to confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

If you cannot sign in

Email itsmexcel@gmail.com from the email address on your account, asking us to delete it. We may need to confirm your identity first. We will reply within one month, and it is free.

What is deleted

Deleted straight away: your sign-in account, your profile, your contact details, your availability, your connections, your settings, your consent records and your listing in the instructor directory. The copy held on your own device is deleted too, and uninstalling the app removes it entirely.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Booking records: 3 years, then permanently deleted. During that time they are linked only to an account ID — not to your name or contact details — so that the instructor or learner you had lessons with keeps an accurate lesson history, and so disputes about lessons can be dealt with.
  • Backups: up to 30 days. Deleted information may remain in our database backups until they are overwritten.

If you want your booking records deleted sooner than the 3 years above, email itsmexcel@gmail.com. We will weigh your request against the other person's interest in keeping their lesson history.
